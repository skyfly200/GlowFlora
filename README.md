# GlowFlora
## GlowFlora Clip Design

### Parts
- [LED Filament](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256806631994581.html)  
- [Battery Holder](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256808616248186.html)  
- [Aligator Clip]()  
- [10CM Origami Paper]()
- [CR2032 Coin Cell Battery]()  
- [Heatshrink Tube]()  
- [Hot Glue]()
- [Twist Tie or 24 AGW Steel Wire]()

### Tools
- Soldering Iron
- Hot Glue Gun
- Scisors
- Heat Gun (optional)
- [Soldering Jig](/LED%20Filament%20soldering%20JIG3.stl)(optional)
- Origami Fold Press (optional)

### Steps 
- Tin the tips of the battery holder wires so they don't fray when we manipulate them
- Twist up battery holder wires with a twist tie leaving about 2CM of twist tie at the base to secure 
- Hot Glue the twist tie to the back side of the battery holder, avoiding interferance with the lid or switch.
- Put heat shrink tube over the twisted wires, cutting it to leave about 5-10mm of wires extending out beyond the tubing. Use a heatgun or a fast moving sweeps with a lighter to shrink the tubing. Be careful not to melt anything! Now you have a stem assembly!
- Fold an origami Lily, optionaly using the press to precrease the fold pattern [Origami Lily Folding Instructions](http://www.origami-instructions.com/origami-lily.html) or using a 3D printed origami "paper" [Origami Lily 3D Model](/Lily_Single%20Color.stl) for a more durable and easy folding version. Using a heat gun with the 3D printed design can help to get the folds to stay and to bend the petals.
- Cut about 5mm off the base of the flower for the wires to slide through
- Cut a 12mm piece of heatshirn tubing and slide it over the stem assembly. DONT FORGET THIS STEP 
- Slide the flower over the stem assembly
- Its a good idea to test the LED polarity first by turning on the switch and holding the wires to the led terminals. This also ensures the LED is working before you solder it.
- Solder the LED filament to the wires. Use the 3D printed jig if you have one to hold the flexable filament and stem assembly in place for this step. Noting the LED polarity from the previous step. It helps if you quickly tin the LED terminals first and bend the stem wires to sit parelell to the terminal in the jig. Be sure to solder quickly as to not damage the LED filament or melt the wires.
- Hot glue over the solder joints and the wire ends for stress relief
- Apply a small buldge of hot glue to the stem right above where the heatshrink ends
- Carefully slide the flower over the molten hot glue so that the glue only connects with its base, lightly press the flower base into the glue so it bonds and sits flush to the stem, and hold it in place while it solidifies.
- Slide the small heatshrink band over the stem flower junction and carefully shrink it with a heatgun or lighter.
- Your Done! Switch it on and admire your work
